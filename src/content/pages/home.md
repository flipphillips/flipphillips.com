My research and teaching focuses on human perception of materials, 3D shape, and motion — mainly through vision and touch.

My background includes undergraduate training in architecture, design, and computer graphics at The Computer Graphics Research Group of The Ohio State University; user interface and medical imaging research and development at OSU and Pixar; animation and technical direction at Pixar Animation Studios[1](javascript:void\(0\))I was the original ‘Animation Scientist’. ; graduate training in environment and behavior, cognitive science, mathematical modeling and vision science at OSU; and teaching in computer graphics, computational methods, psychology, and neuroscience at OSU and Skidmore College.

I am presently teaching [Virtual Production I & II](https://www.rit.edu/news/professor-immersed-grant-epic-games) and [Introduction to Imaging.](https://ritmps.github.io/sofa103/)

Previously, at OSU, I taught Programming for Artists and Introduction to Psychology. At Skidmore, I taught [Computational](https://flipphillips.com/pubs/SyllabusPS327.pdf) and [Quantitative](https://flipphillips.com/pubs/SyllabusPS202.pdf) Methods, [Comparative Vision](https://flipphillips.com/pubs/SyllabusPS312VMM.pdf), [Perception](https://flipphillips.com/pubs/SyllabusPS325.pdf), [Introduction to Psychology,](https://flipphillips.com/pubs/SyllabusPS101.pdf) [Introduction to Cognitive Science](https://flipphillips.com/pubs/SyllabusPS232.pdf) and seminars on [Sensory Compensation](https://flipphillips.com/pubs/SyllabusPS351.pdf), [Sensory Neuroscience](https://flipphillips.com/pubs/SyllabusPSNS312B.pdf), [High Level Vision](https://flipphillips.com/pubs/SyllabusPS312HLV.pdf), [Ecological Perception](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_senses_considered_as_perceptual_syst.html?id=J9ROAAAAMAAJ), and [Computational and Perceptual Aesthetics](https://flipphillips.com/pubs/SyllabusPS251.pdf). I also taught First Year Seminars on [Designing a Mind](https://flipphillips.com/pubs/SyllabusScribner.pdf) and a cross-institution course with Union College on [Psychology and Neuroscience in the Real World](https://flipphillips.com/pubs/SyllabusTeagle.pdf). In addition to my current classes, at RIT I have taught Human Visual Perception, Visual Effects and Compositing, and the [RIT Freshman Imaging Project](https://www.cis.rit.edu/undergraduate-programs/freshman-experience-project).

I am a member of the [Visual Effects Society](https://vesglobal.org) and [Vision Sciences Society](https://www.visionsciences.org) and a past member of ACM-SIGGRAPH, ACM-SIGPLAN, ACM-SIGCHI, Sigma Xi, International Society of Rudimental Percussionists, American Federation of Musicians, Environmental Design Research Association, Society for Neuroscience, APA, APS.

I am incredibly honored to be associated with [Project Prakash.](https://www.projectprakash.org)

I collaborate with folks at [MIT,](https://web.mit.edu/sinhalab/) [OSU,](http://faculty.psy.ohio-state.edu/todd/research.htm) [WKU,](http://people.wku.edu/farley.norman/) [Tel Aviv U](http://english.tau.ac.il/) & [U Gießen.](https://www.allpsych.uni-giessen.de/roland/)

Lastly, I am thankful to be [part machine](https://www.edwards.com/) – titanium, Dacron, and cow.
