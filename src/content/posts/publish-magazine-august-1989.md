A short piece about our wedding announcements, printed on a 100-year-old Heidelberg press via computer graphics.

I posted an [interview from fps Magazine](https://flipphillips.com/bloggery/fps-interview-1995/) a while back, and one of my old Wolfram Era Pals, [Paul Abbott](https://www.linkedin.com/in/paulabbott), asked about the wedding invitation mentioned in that article. I thought I had a copy somewhere, and, indeed I found it in my well curated ‘archives’ (e.g., a Bankers Box in the basement).

![](/images/uploads/2018/08/Wedding-Invite%40033x.png)

This way I’ll never forget our anniversary.

Turns out, Publish Magazine did a story on the work back in 1989, (written by [James A. Martin](https://twitter.com/james_a_martin)) designed by me and printed by [Julie Holcomb Printers](http://www.julieholcombprinters.com). You can [see a PDF of the article here.](https://flipphillips.com/pubs/Publish%20Magazine.pdf) (Be sure to check out the ad from a very early [Casady & Greene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casady_&_Greene) and reminisce about Crystal Quest, Conflict Catcher, and friends.)
