I use _Mathematica_ in many of my classes and all of my research. You may need to use it for some homework and projects.

The versions installed in the Labs and other Public computers at Skidmore are usually a version or two behind. Luckily, we have a site-license for students. Thus, you can get your own, most up-to-date copy.

NOTE that the instructions have changed and have a ‘home’ now at [https://flipphillips.com/mathematica](https://flipphillips.com/mathematica)

Let me know if you have any trouble.
