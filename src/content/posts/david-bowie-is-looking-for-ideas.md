I grew up just down river from [Ann Magnuson](https://annmagnuson.com), one half of [Bongwater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bongwater_\(band\)). They informed the world of David’s needs in [this wonderful song](https://lynkify.in/song/david-bowie-wants-ideas/19228203).

In between leaving Pixar and starting back to grad school, I connected with then-former Pixar buddies [Rob Cook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_L._Cook)[1](#b0bacc44-edd4-4c6c-833d-e0e1e335abb6) and [Ty Roberts](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tyroberts/). Rob is responsible for a variety of great things in RenderMan and it was really nice to see him again a few months ago at the [IEEE RenderMan award.](https://www.rit.edu/aes/news/professor-recognized-role-pixar-pioneer)

[![Flip Phillips and Rob Cook](/images/uploads/2025/07/IMG_2713.jpeg)](/images/uploads/2025/07/IMG_2713-scaled.jpeg?ssl=1)

Me and Rob. I’m the one with glasses. So is he.

[![Pixar RenderMan people at IEEE award for Renderman](/images/uploads/2025/07/renderman-ieee-past-and-present.jpg)](/images/uploads/2025/07/renderman-ieee-past-and-present.jpg?ssl=1)

A lot of people who worked on RenderMan over the decades, at the IEEE award to RenderMan.

Rob and Ty left Pixar to start a company, LightSource. I did some consulting with them during grad school. I worked on some of the original [Apple OneScanner](https://apple.fandom.com/wiki/Apple_OneScanner) software and wrote a nonlinear video editing system for the [NEC PC-VCR](https://archive.org/details/TNM_NEC_Professional_Systems_Division_PC-VCR_Collection_of_Flyers). This was _before_ QuickTime (aka Warhol) was released. Video editing from tape to tape, just like we did with the 3/4″ Sony decks of the era.

As the LightSource mission was coming to an end, Ty spun out a company, [Ion](https://www.facebook.com/ION.MusicTech/posts/in-1992-ion-started-as-a-studio-of-the-future-for-omnidirectional-interactive-an/1090431731038016/), for doing cutting-dege music and media related things. One of the things that I worked on that I really enjoyed was an Enhanced CD for David Bowie called _[Jump](https://www.discogs.com/release/4433398-David-Bowie-Jump-The-David-Bowie-Interactive-CD-ROM)_[2](#c0ddfdda-652f-4331-b04d-4cf26d2c1a79)_._[3](#c1998834-6254-4d76-ae19-0f1645dce5ff) (I posted that I found some of the [code a while back](https://flipphillips.com/bloggery/oh-the-things-youll-find/).) It contained a few tracks from Bowie’s _[Black Tie White Noise](https://www.discogs.com/master/49487-David-Bowie-Black-Tie-White-Noise)_ that you could remix in real time[4](#439a595f-bde1-45db-8926-05ffb4912bc2).

[![](/images/uploads/2025/07/IMG_1175-scaled.jpeg)](/images/uploads/2025/07/IMG_1175-scaled.jpeg?ssl=1)

Not Van Halen.

Check out that amazing interface and the system requirements. The disk had some amazingly good Macromedia Director interactive animation by, um, Roger something whose name I can’t remember now? I wrote the low-level signal processing code for doing the sound. That was my particular domain of expertise.

I met a lot of great people at LightSource and Ion, several I’m still friends with. It was a great way to spend that ‘buffer time’.
