We wrote all the analysis code ourselves in [_Mathematica_](http://www.wolfram.com). There’s a bug in the heat map compositing, but I shall procrastinate a solution soon.

Lifted largely from [Eye Tracking: A comprehensive guide to methods and measures](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0199697086/ref=as_li_tl?ie=UTF8&camp=1789&creative=390957&creativeASIN=0199697086&linkCode=as2&tag=flipphillips-20&linkId=H6QZUWAYWXRVFWNC)![](http://ir-na.amazon-adsystem.com/e/ir?t=flipphillips-20&l=as2&o=1&a=0199697086).

We used some ideas from [Anderson’s](http://link.springer.com/article/10.3758%2Fs13428-012-0299-5) paper on recurrence, modifying them to make a little more sense. We’re still analyzing everything, but we have some interesting results so far.
